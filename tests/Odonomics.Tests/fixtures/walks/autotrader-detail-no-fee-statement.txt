Stock#: KP1140|VIN: JTDBCMFE0T3118000

Meet the 2026 Toyota Corolla Hybrid LE, a practical and efficient sedan designed for today's driver. With its proven hybrid powertrain delivering an impressive 50 city and 43 highway MPG, this vehicle combines fuel economy with dependable performance. The sleek black exterior presents a clean, modern appearance, while the intelligent eCVT transmission ensures smooth acceleration and seamless power delivery.

Key Features:
- 1.8L DOHC 16V VVT Hybrid Engine with eCVT
- 50 City / 43 Highway MPG
- 8 Toyota Audio Multimedia with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto
- AM/FM Radio with SiriusXM
- Automatic Temperature Control with Dual-Zone Capability
- Power Windows, Mirrors, and Steering
- Remote Keyless Entry
- Fully Automatic Headlights with Delay-Off Feature
- Electronic Stability Control and Traction Control
- Rear Parking Camera

This LE trim includes comprehensive safety features to give you peace of mind on every drive. You'll find dual front and side impact airbags, knee airbags, and overhead airbags working in concert with four-wheel disc brakes and ABS. The electronic stability control system actively manages traction, while the Safety Connect emergency communication system provides an added layer of security. The four-wheel independent suspension delivers a comfortable, controlled ride over varied road conditions.

The interior welcomes you with comfortable front bucket seats upholstered in premium fabric, front center armrest, and a split-folding rear seat for flexible storage. Steering wheel-mounted audio controls keep your attention on the road, while the tilt and telescoping steering wheel accommodates drivers of all sizes. The trip computer, tachometer, and outside temperature display provide useful driving information at a glance. Illuminated entry and front reading lights add convenience to your daily routine.

Experience this efficient and well-equipped Corolla Hybrid in person visit us today to take it for a test drive and discover why Toyota continues to lead in reliability and value.

Our goal is for you to feel that the vehicle you drive away in is the perfect one for you.
